Case study 01 · Noma · Wellness

A calmer kind of
daily ritual.

Building a distinct, feel-good identity for an everyday energy brand that puts balance before buzz.

Challenge

Make a new wellness drink stand apart in a noisy category without leaning on the usual performance clichés.

Our approach

We centered the story on a gentler energy ritual, then translated that feeling into warm color, tactile product imagery, and clear, human language.

Brand strategy · Naming direction · Visual identity · Packaging system · E-commerce concept · Launch campaign
